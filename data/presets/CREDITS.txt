JK Studio - preset library (data/presets)

The animations in this folder come from other authors. JK Studio ships them on
their own skeletons (BVH, cut to one cycle and made in place by tools/presetbau);
they are transferred to your figure only inside JK Studio, with the retargeting.
No file of any game is part of this library.

100style/*.bvh
  The 100STYLE Dataset - Ian Mason, https://www.ianxmason.com/100style/
  (Zenodo record 8127870, 100STYLE.zip)
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: one walking cycle cut from the forward-walk take (Frame_Cuts.csv FW range), resampled from
  60 to 30 frames per second, root moved in place, seam spread over the cycle.
  Paper: I. Mason, S. Starke, T. Komura, "Real-Time Style Modelling of Human Locomotion via Feature-Wise
  Transformations and Local Motion Phases", Proc. ACM Comput. Graph. Interact. Tech. 5(1), 2022,
  doi:10.1145/3522618.
