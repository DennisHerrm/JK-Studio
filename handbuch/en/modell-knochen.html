Model tab: bones, rig styles and see-through

Bones look like in the program you come from: bone boxes like 3ds Max, octahedra, sticks, B-bones, envelopes or wires like Blender. The figure can become see-through, bones and controls can lie in front of it or inside it, and the control rig comes in four looks. None of this changes what the rig calculates.

How bones are drawn#

Bones show with Bones (view header; in the area Weights they are on by default). Like 3ds Max a bone is a bone box that narrows towards its end, filled as in the design; Bone fins adds its fins. Like Blender Display as chooses the display as in Blender's Armature > Viewport Display: Octahedral (default), Stick (a line of constant width with dots at the joints), B-Bone (a box of constant width), Envelope (a sphere at each joint, joined along the bone) and Wire (a thin line). The choice is kept. The selected bone is highlighted; bones that the game code looks for (cranium, pelvis, the hands ...) keep their orange mark. With the tool Select bone a click anywhere on the drawn bone selects it, not only near its joint.

One clean chain per limb: every bone is exactly one box (an octahedron like Blender) from its joint along its own axis to the next joint of its chain, as 3ds Max bones; twist helpers such as lradiusX and the root are small points (Show helper bones shows them as boxes), and a thin line links the start of a chain to its parent. Foot platform like CAT: with a control rig each leg has a flat plate under the foot on the floor; moving and turning it leads the IK leg, the knee follows its target, and the pivots heel, ball and toe roll the foot.

In front and see-through#

In front draws bones and controls over the figure, as Blender's In Front does; this is the default. Switched off, the figure hides what lies inside or behind it, as in 3ds Max. To look inside then, make the figure see-through: like 3ds Max See-through with Alt+X - it belongs to the figure and shows in all views; like Blender X-ray with Alt+Z - it belongs to the active view, and bones stand in front of the figure there. The figure is drawn with half opacity, its front surface once. Both switches are in the shading menu of the view header ([Standard] like 3ds Max, Viewport Shading like Blender), in the group Bone and rig display on the right in the area Animation, in the right-click menu of the view and in Search all actions.

Rig styles#

Rig style chooses how the control rig and the skeleton look - in the group Bone and rig display on the right in the area Animation, in the bone menu of the view header, in the right-click menu of the view and in Search all actions. Standard is the look of the design: squares (like 3ds Max) or rings (like Blender) at the joints, coloured by side. Control rig (rings) looks like a control rig in 3ds Max: rings around the body parts that fit the figure, foot plates, poles as spheres with a line to the joint, a circle on the ground; right is red, left is blue, the middle green and yellow, and IK or FK stands at each limb. CAT boxes looks like a CAT rig: a box per bone in the measures and colours of CAT (hips orange, chest yellow, head red, left arm green, right arm blue, legs dark green and dark blue), foot platforms, IK targets at the hands, crosses as pole targets and the shape on the ground; a box is the FK control of its bone. Blender octahedra looks like an IK rig in Blender: octahedra, circles around hips and spine, squares at hands and feet, circles as pole targets; a bone is its own FK control. A click selects the shape you see - the ring, the box (its edges and its inside), the circle; then you move or rotate the control as always. The style changes only the look: IK, FK, keys and the files written for the game stay the same.
