Model tab: the timeline

The timeline under the views works in every area of the Model tab and looks like the one of the chosen controls: time slider, track bar, Auto Key and Set Key like 3ds Max, or the Timeline like Blender.

Layout in every area#

The timeline lies under the views across the whole width, in every area - the pose keeps playing while you model, unwrap, weight or paint. From top to bottom: the handle, the button row, the scale with the frame numbers and the playhead (a line with the frame number in a box), the tracks, a line with the legend and at the very bottom the status line with the controls (a click switches between like 3ds Max and like Blender), the keys of the area and undo/search. A click or drag on the numbers of the scale sets the frame. The area decides how much is shown: Animation has the full timeline with the keys of the selection in the scale and one track per limb and bone; View/Check has the speed (− 1.00× +), Interpolation, Force loop and the two lines for the primary and the secondary sequence; the other areas have the short timeline. Tracks or the handle (drag it up or down) opens and closes the tracks and sets their height; next to it you switch between Dope sheet and Curve editor. Everything else is in the Timeline menu (the button with the three lines at the end of the row, or a right-click on the timeline) and in the search X/F3.

Like 3ds Max#

With the controls like 3ds Max the button row has To start, Previous frame, Play, Next frame, To end, the frame display counted from 0 (12 / 119), Auto Key (N), Set Key (K) and the button with the frame rate, which opens the Time configuration. The scale and every track show a fine line per frame, like the track bar of 3ds Max. Keys are bars in the colours of the track bar: red for movement, green for rotation, both halves for a key that does both - JKA stores no scaling, so there is no blue. The row Selection (track bar) in the scale carries the keys of the shown bones: click a key to select it, drag it to move it in time, drag a frame around keys to select several. With Key mode (timeline menu or time configuration) Previous frame, Next frame and the keys , and . jump from key to key.

Like Blender#

With the controls like Blender the row has To start, Previous key (↓), Play (Space), Next key (↑), To end, the record button for Auto keying, the fields Start, End and Frame counted from 1, and the frame rate of the animation.cfg. Keys are diamonds, the row in the scale is called Summary. Start and End are the first frame and the count of the sequence in the animation.cfg - a change there changes that line (undo with Ctrl+Z). In the area Animation K opens the keying menu and I sets a key; with the mouse over the tracks Home shows the whole sequence. The timeline menu is grouped like the header of Blender's timeline: Playback, Keying, View, Marker.

Auto Key and Set Key#

Auto Key (like Blender Auto keying) is off from the start, as in 3ds Max and Blender: you pose first - the pose stays without a key, the line under the timeline says so, and Set Key (K, like Blender I) keeps it. If you change the frame or the sequence before that, the pose is discarded - as in the Set Key mode of 3ds Max and in Blender without auto keying. Switched on (the button, N like 3ds Max, the record button like Blender), every change at a control - the gizmo, G/R, the sliders of IK/FK and of the foot roll - sets a key at the current frame. The program remembers your choice for the next start. Commands such as paste pose, bind pose and the hand poses always set their keys.

Time configuration#

The Time configuration shows the frame rate, switches the time display between Frames and Seconds (scale and playhead), holds the speed, Force loop, Interpolation and Key mode, and the line of the sequence in the animation.cfg: first frame, count, loop frame and fps with the bar whose ends you can drag. The skin list and the surface counter that stood in the timeline until rc611 are in the group Level of detail and skin of the area View/Check; the surface list for the detail log is also in the search.
