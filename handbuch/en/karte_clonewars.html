Map and scene

Map editor: test figure, scale and clip tools

Tools from the requirements of a Clone Wars mod: a player figure in original size, models imported with the right scale and axes, rooms scaled with a note in the map, smooth collision for stairs and walls. Everything is off by default or acts only when you call it.

Test figure#

View > Test figure > Show test figure (F7, or the button at the end of the toolbar) shows the player in original size in the 2D views and the camera: the box the engine moves (32 x 32 x 64 in single player and Movie Duels, 30 wide in multiplayer; the origin at hip height, the feet 24 below; crouched the top is at 16 instead of 40 - OpenJK g_client.cpp and bg_public.h), the STEPSIZE mark 18 above the feet, the other height, and frames for the minimum door and corridor of the optimizer. In the camera the game's player model stands in it, posed like the game when the player stands still (crouched the held last frame of the crouch animation).

Shift+F7 puts the figure under the mouse pointer: in a 2D view on the two axes of the view (the depth stays), in the camera with its feet on the surface. A right click in a view offers Test figure here, a right click on the toolbar button the whole menu. Off by default; the choices are remembered.

Import with scale and axes#

The dialog of File > Import geometry has a Template: Default (as before) reads the file exactly as before (the default), Battlefield 3 / Y up turns Y up into Z up as (x, -z, y), Battlefront 2 / Z up keeps the axes, Source / GMod (x 0.889) keeps them and scales by 64 / 72 (the player is 72 units tall in Source SDK 2013, 64 here), Custom axes lets you choose the file axis for X, Y and Z. Flip winding / normals is for models whose triangles run the other way round (Empire at War). The dialog shows the size in units and in test figures, Height = test figure scales a character model to 64, and a front view shows the model next to the test figure. The template is remembered; it also applies when you drop a file onto a view.

Clip tools and the layer Collision#

Modify > Clone Wars tools (also at the top of the right-click menu of a view when something is selected) works on the selection, one undo step each:

  • Stairs to clip ramp: select stairs made of brushes or a misc_model. A smooth ramp of textures/system/clip (player and NPCs, shots still hit the steps) runs through the front edges of the steps, from the bottom of the stairs to the top step. The visible steps lose their collision the way q3map2 understands it: a hidden side (caulk, otherwise the bottom) becomes system/nodraw, which is non-solid, and one non-solid side makes the whole brush passable; a model loses its clip spawnflags; pure collision brushes of the selection are removed. Built with q3map2, the player walks up without stepping; the optimizer check sight against collision reports nothing there. A ramp steeper than walkable (MIN_WALK_NORMAL 0.7) gets a warning.
  • Smooth clip wall (plane) / Smooth clip wall (convex hull): select an irregular wall (brushes, patches or a misc_model). The plane variant puts a clip block along the wall whose front lies at the point that sticks out most, on the side of the camera; the hull variant uses the convex hull of the selection.
  • Scale room: scales the selection (a room) by a factor about the center of the selection at the bottom; point entities move with it, models keep their size. An info_notnull at that point gets cw_scale (and cw_room), and the .map gets a comment line "// cw_scale ..." - data only, the game and q3map2 ignore both. Scale and save as part then saves the room with its marker as its own part (File > Save selected).
  • Layer Collision: the clip brushes of these tools - and of "Model + clip" with the check profile Clone Wars chain - go into their own layer named Collision. It only appears when one of them is used; Show layer Collision in the same menu (or the Layers browser) shows and hides it.
