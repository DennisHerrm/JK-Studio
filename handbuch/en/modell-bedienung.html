Missions

Model tab: controls like 3ds Max or Blender

Choose the controls you know - the Model tab then uses the keys, views and bone display of 3ds Max or of Blender. It has no script parts; animations go into a script through the window Into script.

Switch between like 3ds Max and like Blender with a click on the control mode at the left of the status line of the tab, in the menu of the header of a view (Controls), in the settings or with the search for all actions; the setting is remembered and acts at once - on the keys, the mouse and the layout (Model tab: the layout like Blender). Like 3ds Max: four views (Alt+W), bone objects, Q/W/E/R with the gizmo, sub-object levels 1 vertex, 2 edge, 3 border, 4 polygon, 5 element, 6 object, the timeline from 0. Like Blender: one view (Ctrl+Alt+Q for four), octahedral bones, G/R/S with X/Y/Z (twice: local, Shift: plane) and a typed number, Enter or left click confirms, Esc or right click cancels; Tab toggles object and edit mode, 1/2/3 vertex, edge, face, the numpad views 1/3/7 (Ctrl for the opposite side), 5 perspective, 2/4/6/8 orbit by 15°, Home frames all, Space plays, the timeline counts from 1 and shows the GLA frame in its tooltip. X (3ds Max) or F3 (Blender) searches all actions; the right mouse button opens the menu of the view.

Right-click in a view like 3ds Max: the quad menu. Four quadrants meet at the mouse: transform at the bottom right (Move, Rotate, Scale, Select, Clone, Curve Editor, Dope Sheet ...), display at the top right (Isolate Selection, Unfreeze All, Freeze Selection, Unhide All, Hide Unselected, Hide Selection), tools 1 at the top left (the sub-object levels and the tools of the area) and tools 2 at the bottom left (display, weights, animation, script, measure). Move, Rotate and Scale set the tool like W, E and R; the active tool is marked. Submenus open outwards - to the left in the left quadrants, to the right in the right ones; at the edge of the window the whole quad menu moves inwards. Like Blender the right-click opens the context menu of the mode, with Transform (G, R, S) at the top.

Esc closes every right-click menu of the Model tab (view, Scene Explorer, timeline and dope sheet, command panel, toolbar) and the menus of the menu bar, also from an open submenu, as in 3ds Max and Blender; nothing else happens with that key press. Without an open menu Esc does what it did before, for example it ends a navigation mode.

Controls like 3ds Max#

like 3ds Max is the default. The keys work after a click into the view, the explorer, the command panel or the timeline; X lists them all. The gizmo moves, rotates or scales the selected surfaces in the model document (all levels of detail) - Ctrl+Z takes it back.

Animations into a script#

The Model tab is only for models: no script tree, no button column, no compile status; File > Open opens a model and Ctrl+S does not touch the script. To put an animation into a script, open Into script (the button with the script symbol in the head of the scene explorer, right-click on a sequence, the menu File or Ctrl+Shift+I). Choose the script (open ones or a new one), the character for an affect block (empty: none) and the command - SET_ANIM_BOTH/UPPER/LOWER or a hold time with the length of the animation; the preview shows the line, Insert puts it in with one undo step. The small button in the animation field takes the animation selected in the scene explorer; Enter inserts, Esc or Cancel closes the window. While the window is closed it takes no space.
