Missions

Model tab: control rig, poses, ghosts and paths

Recognise the bones, create a control rig with one click, pose with FK, copy and mirror poses, see ghosts and motion paths and bake the result into a GLA.

The rig with one click#

In the area Animation, Create rig (also in the menu Animation, in the right-click of the view and in the search X/F3) recognises every bone: its role (pelvis, spine, neck, head, clavicle, upper arm, forearm, hand, fingers, thigh, calf, foot, toes, twist bones, tag bones, face) and its side. The name gives a first answer (pelvis, lhumerus, r_d1_j1, L_thigh, leg_left_bone01 ...); the hierarchy and the bind pose check it - legs hang where the limbs reach lowest, the parts of a limb follow each other outwards, the spine rises from the pelvis to the head, the side comes from the side axis of the skeleton. Where name and position disagree, the position wins and the bone is marked unclear. It works for the skeletons of Jedi Academy (53 bones), Jedi Outcast (72, everything flat under pelvis and thoracic) and the creatures and droids of Movie Duels. The rig then has controls for every part of the body, pole targets for elbows and knees, the foot roll (heel, ball, toe tip) and the saber grip at *r_hand; pole targets and foot roll work with IK (page IK/FK).

The overview: assign by clicking#

Rig overview shows a figure: green spots are recognised, yellow ones unclear, grey ones empty; a number says how many bones share the role (fingers, spine). Hover a spot for its bones and the reason when it is unclear. Click a spot, then a bone in Unclear and without role or in Assign bone - the bone takes that role. The assignment is part of the model document (Ctrl+Z) and the rig is rebuilt at once.

Posing: FK like 3ds Max and like Blender#

The controls stand in the scene explorer under Control rig (own symbols, the eye hides one in the view) and in the views - like 3ds Max as boxes, like Blender as rings, left blue, right green, middle yellow. Click one in the view or in the explorer. Like 3ds Max, E shows the rotation gizmo and W moves the pelvis; like Blender, R rotates (X/Y/Z for an axis, click or Enter to confirm, Esc to cancel) and G moves the pelvis. Every rotation is FK the way the game turns a head or a torso (G2API_SetBoneAngles), and a key at the current frame of the selected sequence; between two keys the pose is interpolated. Bones that belong to the hand but do not hang below it in the skeleton (the fingers of Jedi Academy under rradius, the whole arm of Jedi Outcast) turn with it, and the twist bone (rradiusX) takes the share of the twist that matches its place on the forearm. Keys do not rebuild the mesh; Ctrl+Z takes each one back.

Poses, mirroring and hand poses#

Copy pose remembers the pose of every bone as you see it, Paste pose sets it as keys at another frame, Paste mirrored with left and right swapped and mirrored at the side axis of the skeleton (like Blender: Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+Shift+V). Reset to bind pose (Blender Alt+R) and Whole figure to bind pose go back to the bind pose of the GLA. The hand poses come from the animations of the game itself: open is the bind pose, fist the first frame of BOTH_MELEE1, saber grip the first frame of BOTH_STAND1. To select, Select parent and Select child walk the skeleton (3ds Max Page Up / Page Down, Blender [ and ]), Select opposite jumps to the bone of the other side (Blender Ctrl+Shift+M).

Ghosts, silhouette and motion paths#

Ghosts shows the poses before (blue) and after (orange) the current frame see-through - how many and how far apart is set under Before, After and Step, the colours below. Silhouette draws the figure black on a light background to judge how readable a pose is. Path for selection shows the path of the selected bone or tag (*r_hand for the saber, a foot, the root) over the frames before and after: a dot per frame, the current frame white, keys as squares. Ghosts and paths stay inside the sequence and follow every key at once.

Baking into the GLA#

Bake into the GLA writes the selected sequence with the keys of the rig as GLA frames and puts it on the next free slot of the target in Target GLA and slots (map GLA, _humanoid.gla or an own GLA). Bones without a key keep the 14 bytes of the original GLA; changed ones are compressed like Carcass (compressBone). A rig without changes bakes byte for byte the original - checked with every GLA of Jedi Academy, Jedi Outcast and Movie Duels; compressing everything anew stays within two quantisation steps per quaternion component.
