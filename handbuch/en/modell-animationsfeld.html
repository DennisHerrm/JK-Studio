Missions

Model tab: the panel of the area Animation

The right panel of the area Animation shows its groups in the order of the work: the sequence and its target, the rig, presets, editing an existing animation, transfer from another skeleton, IK/FK, attachments and building for the game.

The groups and their tabs#

On the tab that the area chooses (Motion like 3ds Max, Bone like Blender) all groups stand one below the other: Animation, Create rig, Presets, Edit existing animation, Transfer (retargeting), IK/FK, Attachments and preview, Build for the game, and behind them Auto physics, Pose, Ghosts, Motion paths, Curves and animation tools, Pose library, GLA (skeleton and animation), animation.cfg and Target GLA and slots. A click on a heading folds the group. The other tabs show their part: Hierarchy the rig, IK/FK and the attachments, Utilities building for the game, the pose library, GLA, animation.cfg and the slots (like Blender: Constraints and Data). Animation names the sequence you work on, the target GLA and how many of its slots are taken. Dope sheet and curve editor open in the timeline.

Setup Mode and Animation Mode like CAT: the switch at the top of Motion (Ctrl+Shift+M; like Blender Edit Mode / Pose Mode with Tab) splits the groups. Setup holds creating the rig, attachments, building for the game, GLA, animation.cfg, target GLA and slots; the figure stands in the base pose of the GLA and no keys are set - K, Auto Key, G and R say why. Animation holds the sequence, presets, editing, transfer, IK/FK, auto physics, pose, motion paths, curves and the pose library. Ghosts and the bone display are on the tab Display. After loading, the mode is Animation when the figure has sequences or a rig, otherwise Setup. As in 3ds Max, bones are visible, selectable and rotatable from the start, without a control rig: a click selects the bone (also through the skin), E rotates it, Set Key and Auto Key key it.

Create rig: templates#

The table shows the skeleton, its scale (fScale) and the template it was recognised as. There are templates for the skeletons of the base game - human, rancor, wampa, tauntaun, howler, AT-ST, droids, vehicles and ships - and for rigs of other programs. The choice below sets another template or Own skeleton (the chains are then suggested and can be changed in the Rig overview). The template also names the chains of the target when you transfer an animation. Create control rig builds the rig with IK targets, poles and foot rolls; Animation rig (FK only) builds one with rotation controls only. Like a CAT rig, Create control rig also puts an animation of the sequence onto the rig from FK to IK: the frames become keys on the controls, hands and feet follow their IK targets and poles, the motion looks the same (the message names the largest deviation). Then you work on the controls - move the hand or foot target, the pole (elbow, knee) or roll the foot - at any frame; Put on rig does the same for an animation loaded later.

Animation rig (FK only) after Create control rig: limbs that are on IK in a sequence are first baked into FK keys at every frame (in every sequence of the figure), then the IK tracks are removed - the motion stays exactly the same and every rotation control turns its bone. Ctrl+Z brings the IK tracks, the IK targets and poles back in one step. Create control rig turns the IK targets on again and puts the limbs back on IK in every sequence - their FK keys go to IK targets and poles like the first time; one Ctrl+Z takes it back.

Presets and existing animations#

Presets: the eight categories of the design (Idle, Walk, Run, Backwards, Crouch, Jump, Combat, Gestures) always stand there, behind them Other; a category without presets yet shows a short note. Choose a preset in the list - source and licence stand next to it. In Walk and Run the list also has Walk (generated) and Run (generated) from the own generator: chosen, Speed and Arm swing stand under the list, Generator shows the rest (posture, step length, hip swing, bounce, measuring). Preview on figure loads or generates it, transfers it to the skeleton of the figure and plays it; Put on figure (retarget) puts it on the Target slot; right-click on an entry offers the same. Edit existing animation: Reduce frames to keys turns the frames of the sequence into keys (the options limit it to the selected bones or a span), Bake into the GLA writes the edited sequence back as frames of a GLA onto a slot of the target. Transfer (retargeting): choose the source or import an animation, check the chains in the table, then the main button puts the result on the slot; Foot sliding shows how far the feet slide.

IK/FK, attachments, building#

IK/FK has one line per limb. Like 3ds Max the button on the right shows the state and changes it (grab a hand or a foot = IK, rotate a bone = FK) - like the IK/FK of a CAT limb from the current frame up to the next switch, and it first matches the other side (Match IK and FK), so the pose does not jump; like Blender there is a slider from IK to FK and the two buttons that snap without a jump. Below it you set the space, the foot roll and the keys of the chosen limb. Attachments and preview lists what hangs on the figure and what it plays: the attached models, the primary and the secondary sequence. Build for the game shows the Root travel - how far the pelvis travels over the floor in the sequence - and builds the target GLA with its animation.cfg. The game plays this movement but does not move the origin of the figure: a script has to set SET_ORIGIN together with the follow-up animation.
